Mine Your Own Backyard Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AuCuAg Labs (아우쿠악랩스, "we") handles personal data in the Google Play edition of the mobile game "Mine Your Own Backyard" (Korean title: 뒤뜰에서 보물캐기, the "Game").

Which version applies: This English version and its Japanese and Traditional Chinese translations apply to users of the Google Play edition who live outside the Republic of Korea, including Section 11 for your country. For users in Korea, the Korean Privacy Policy (https://dkim453.github.io/daebak-mine/privacy.ko.html) is the original; it also covers the other editions of the Game (Apps in Toss and ONE store), which are offered only in Korea. If a translation differs from this English version, this English version prevails unless the law of your country requires otherwise.

At a glance

  • There is no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email address. The only exceptions are the email you send us with a question, and the bank name, account number and account holder's name we receive when Google Play cannot refund a withdrawn purchase and we refund it to you directly (Section 2).
  • When you first open the Game, an account without a name (an "anonymous account") is created automatically, and your progress is stored on our servers in Seoul, Republic of Korea, separately from the other editions of the Game.
  • If your device is signed in to Google Play Games, we keep a one-way hashed Play Games player identifier, used to restore your progress on another device. We never receive your Play Games name, email address or photo.
  • Your nickname, guild name and notice, rankings and scores, and chat messages (when chat is open) are visible to other players.
  • Payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card number or other payment details.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob and by Unity Ads through AdMob (Section 4).
  • You can delete your account in the Game (Settings > Delete account) or by following https://dkim453.github.io/daebak-mine/delete.html (Section 7).

6. How long we keep data

The retention periods are in Section 2. When a period ends or the purpose is achieved, we delete the data so that it cannot be restored. Data that the law requires us to keep (such as payment records for 5 years under the Korean E-Commerce Act) is separated from other data and deleted when the required period ends. Our server provider does not keep automatic backups of the database (free plan).

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

  1. In the Game: Settings > Delete account. The screen shows your Account ID; please write it down. Your account is blocked right away and disappears from the Game and from other players' view, and 30 days later we delete its game data and its login account. Until then you can cancel by emailing us with your Account ID.
  2. Without the app: follow https://dkim453.github.io/daebak-mine/delete.html, or email aucuaglabs@gmail.com with your Account ID (Settings in the Game). If you no longer have the app but bought something, send your Google order number (it starts with "GPA.") instead. You do not need to reinstall the app.

When we delete an account, we erase its progress, nickname, guild membership, chat messages, reports, blocks, sanctions, survey answers, key game events, play statistics and the Play Games link. We keep only: payment records (5 years, separated from the account), error reports (which have no account number, 30 days) and server logs (up to 7 days).

If you have neither an Account ID nor an order number, we cannot tell which anonymous account is yours. Such accounts are deleted automatically after the inactivity period in Section 2 if they never bought anything.

8. Your rights

Depending on the law where you live, you may ask us to let you see your data, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, stop or limit processing, or withdraw consent you gave (for example, for the survey or notifications). You may also object to how we handle your data and complain to your data protection authority (Section 11).

To make a request, email aucuaglabs@gmail.com with your Account ID. We may ask for a Google order number to confirm the account is yours. Requests are free. We reply within 10 days, or sooner if your law requires it. If we cannot do what you ask, we tell you why.

9. How we protect data

  • All connections between the app and our servers use HTTPS. Each player can read and write only their own account's data on the server.
  • Chat messages are stored encrypted. The key is kept in a separate secret store on the server.
  • The Play Games player identifier is stored only as a one-way hash that cannot be turned back into the original identifier.
  • Only the operator uses the admin tools, server accounts and store consoles, with strong passwords and two-step verification.
  • If a data breach happens, we will notify the authorities and affected users as the law requires (Section 11).

10. Children

The Google Play edition is not made for children under 13 and is not listed for them on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). Please do not put personal data in nicknames, guild notices or chat. If we learn that a child's data has been processed, we delete it without delay at the request of the child's parent or guardian.
